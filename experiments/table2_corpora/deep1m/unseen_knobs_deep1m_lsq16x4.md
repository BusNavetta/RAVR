1,000,000 items, LSQ16x4, seed 17, 400 test queries, candidate depth 120,000. Every cell is one calibration; RAVR itself is unchanged throughout.

`ravr` can only reach the cap when `coverage >= (m-8)/8`: 0.25 at cap 10, 0.50 at cap 12, 0.75 at cap 14. That bound is **necessary, not sufficient** -- some seen items also carry a zero risk gap, and the bisection plus one-step repair does not reach the combinatorial optimum.
